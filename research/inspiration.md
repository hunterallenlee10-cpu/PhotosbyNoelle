# Inspiration: Photos by Noelle

Captured 12 Oct 2026 at 1440x900 (plus one scrolled view) and 390x844. Shots are in `research/shots/` (JPEG, quality 72). "Confirmed" means checked on Awwwards listings. **Rule: we take principles, never layouts, code or assets.**

### 01 Amy Currell: amycurrell.com
- **Award:** Awwwards SOTD + Developer Award, 12 Nov 2023 (confirmed, but older than 18 months).
- **Not captured.** A Cloudflare human-verification check blocked the browser (not bypassed). Notes come from the page markup only.
- **Pattern:** A numbered index of media ("01 Motion / 02 Stills / 03 Concept") above a long, uncaptioned image sequence.
- **For Noelle:** Make the two sides equal, numbered peers: "01 Game Day / 02 Golden Hour". Do not copy two things: her "Click to enter" gate, and her ~50 images with empty alt text.

### 02 Ethan W: ethanwong.photography
- **Award:** Awwwards HM, 15 Sep 2026 (confirmed, and shown in the site footer).
- **Pattern:** The navigation is large serif type over a full-bleed photo. Hovering "COMMERCIAL" turned the word red, added a "/ PRODUCT & PLACE" sub-label and swapped the background to that category's image.
- **Mobile:** The same list, plus a floating bottom tab bar with a red "Inquire" pill in thumb reach.
- **For Noelle:** Use "Baseball / Field Hockey / Soccer / Seniors / Couples" as the index, with a hover preview, and a bottom bar on mobile with a "Book" pill. Unlike Ethan's ~50% dark overlay, put a scrim only behind the text.

### 03 RemyShoots: remyshoots.co.za
- **Award:** Awwwards HM, 12 Sep 2026 (confirmed).
- **Pattern:** Camera controls used as real UI:
  - The active slide is in colour and its neighbours are desaturated.
  - A tick ruler like a focus scale runs along the bottom, with a red index pointer marking the position.
  - Counters: "WORKS(21) STILLS(13) MOTION(8)" and "STILLS[9]".
  - The client logo and the "VIEW PROJECT" cursor sit inside crop brackets.
  - On mobile the ruler becomes a rotary dial.
- **For Noelle:** A gallery scrubber with a "FR 07/36" counter, and a crop-bracket cursor. Skip their "Enter with sound" gate.

## What we will NOT do
- **Gates or preloaders before the first photo** (RemyShoots, Bruty, The Immortals, Amy Currell). The hero photo should be the page's largest content and paint first, with LCP under 2.5s.
- **A first screen without a photo** (Rys, Xanvier Allison, Maria Vasilyeva's mobile).
- **Centre-cropping action on mobile** (Adam Pretty). Every image gets a focal point, and the ball and the athlete always stay in frame.
- **Dead mobile viewport** (Darren Carroll's ~65% blank space).
- **UI, logos or giant numerals over faces** (Nick Ho's "98", Christie's corner name, Bruty's logo plate).
- **Dimming the whole photo** to make type readable (Ethan W). Noelle's bright colour editing is what she sells.
- **Tiny thumbnails** as the main way to browse (Maria Vasilyeva).
- **Scroll-jacking.** Rys appears to turn vertical scroll into horizontal movement; we keep native scroll.
- **Sound on by default, and empty alt text.**
- **Clipped or low-contrast type** (Bruty's "ENTER" runs off the edge).
